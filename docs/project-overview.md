# 📖 Project Overview

## What is CampusSwap?

**CampusSwap** is a college-focused student marketplace platform that enables students to buy, sell, exchange, and donate used items within their campus community.

## Who is it for?

- 🎓 College students looking to **sell** items they no longer need
- 🛒 Students wanting to **buy** items at affordable prices
- 🔄 Students who want to **exchange** items with peers
- 💝 Students willing to **donate** items to fellow students

## What can be traded?

| Category | Examples |
|---|---|
| 📚 Books & Notes | Textbooks, notebooks, study guides |
| 🖩 Electronics | Calculators, laptops, earphones |
| 🏠 Hostel Items | Mattresses, fans, study lamps |
| 🎒 Stationery | Pens, folders, drawing tools |
| 👕 Clothing | Lab coats, uniforms |
| 🔧 Tools | Instruments, project kits |

## Why CampusSwap?

Unlike general marketplaces (OLX, Facebook Marketplace), CampusSwap is:

- **Campus-specific** — only verified students can join
- **Trustworthy** — college email verification
- **Focused** — built around student needs
- **Safe** — admin moderation and reporting system
