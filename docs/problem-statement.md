# ❓ Problem Statement

## The Core Problem

Students accumulate items during their college journey — textbooks, electronics, hostel essentials — that become unused after a semester or upon graduation. Simultaneously, newer students need these exact items but cannot afford brand-new prices.

## Current Situation

Today, students rely on highly informal and inefficient channels:

| Channel | Problems |
|---|---|
| 📱 WhatsApp Groups | Messages get lost, no search, no categories |
| 👥 Friends & Word of Mouth | Very limited reach |
| 📌 Notice Boards | Offline only, no images, hard to update |
| 🌐 Random Social Media Groups | Unverified users, spam, unsafe |
| 🗣️ Offline Communication | Slow, time-consuming, limited audience |

## Key Pain Points

### For Sellers
- No dedicated platform to list items
- Difficult to reach the right buyers
- No way to verify buyer identity

### For Buyers
- Cannot easily discover available items
- No price comparison
- Trust issues with unknown sellers
- Have to actively ask around instead of browsing

### For the Campus Community
- Usable items end up wasted
- New students overspend on brand-new items
- No sense of campus-wide resource sharing

## The Impact

- 💸 Students overspend on new items when used ones are readily available
- 🗑️ Perfectly usable items are discarded
- ⏱️ Hours wasted in informal searching and negotiation
- 🔒 Safety risks from dealing with unverified strangers online

## Our Solution

**CampusSwap** — a dedicated, verified, and organized student marketplace that solves all of the above pain points in one platform.
